# Proof / Verification of Employment (VOE) & Official Tax/Government Fallback Guide

> **Core Contractor / Employer-of-Record (EOR) Rule:** For contingent, agency, or vendor engagements (Apple 2014–2015, Infoblox via Acclaro, Apple 2026 via MindSource / WPP), **the staffing agency / Employer of Record issues the VOE**, not the client company. Client HR systems typically have no direct payroll record for contingent workers and will reject third-party or alumni verification requests.
>
> **Historical Records Caveat (7–10+ Years):** Corporate HR retention windows and entity restructurings frequently limit automated verification for roles from 2006–2016. For those roles, pair corporate/GDPR DSAR requests with **Part B (Official Government / Tax & Social Security Records)**.
