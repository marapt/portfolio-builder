## 📅 Sprint Cadence & Decision Protocol

### How Decisions Are Made
1. **Agent proposes** → Findings synced to `backend/data/local_db.json`
2. **Dashboard surfaces** → You (and I) review the finding in Governance HQ.
3. **Human approves or rejects** → Decisions synced to Jira & LocalDB.
4. **Execution proceeds** → Site status updates to "CLEARED" only after sign-off.

## 🔑 Core Principles (Non-Negotiable)

1. **Human-in-the-loop always** — No agent acts without your pre-approval.
2. **Slow is smooth, smooth is fast** — No shortcuts in legal, compliance, or brand.
3. **Evidence before claims** — Every value proposition must be backed by proof.
4. **One market at a time** — Perfect EN + pt-PT before any new locale.
5. **Reversible decisions first** — Always stage before shipping to production.
6. **Strict Jira Governance** — Every Phase, Epic, and Sprint discussed locally must be natively minted in Atlassian Jira before software execution begins.
