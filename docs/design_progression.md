## 🛠️ Lessons in Scaling Design
- **Tokenization**: By moving to HSL variables in `index.css`, we ensured that any future brand pivots can be implemented site-wide in under 60 seconds.
- **Typography as Authority**: The switch to **Outfit** immediately moved the brand from "Generic Developer" to "Program Management Strategist."
- **Visual Contrast**: Using dark-mode elements even in light-mode backgrounds (like the new Header) provides a premium, modern software-as-a-service (SaaS) feel.
